/faq

Common questions

Who can use Ideamart?

Anyone. Developers, entrepreneurs, students and businesses can create an account and start building with telco APIs today.

Which mobile networks can I reach?

Ideamart gives you access to subscribers on Dialog, Hutch and Airtel through one account and the same APIs.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. Ideamart works on a revenue share model. There is no upfront payment. When your service earns revenue, you keep 70% and Ideamart retains 30%.

What is the difference between IdeaPro and IdeaApp?

IdeaPro is for developers who build their own application on the APIs. IdeaApp is for content providers who want ready-made services such as Alert, Contact and Voting without writing code.

How do I go live?

Create an account, provision your application, submit it for approval, test in limited production, then move to active production.

Which phone number formats do the APIs accept?

The preferred format is tel:+94771234567. The MCP tools also accept +94771234567, 94771234567 and 0771234567.

Where can I get help?

Email info@ideamart.io, message us on WhatsApp at +94 76 741 2345, or visit us at 475 Union Place, Colombo 02.

about ideamart

Opening the network to everyone.

Ideamart is a developer ecosystem and API marketplace presented by Dialog Axiata PLC. Since 2012 we have given developers, entrepreneurs and businesses access to mobile networks through shared APIs. Today one Ideamart account reaches subscribers on Dialog, Hutch and Airtel.

Our mission

Spark national progress by empowering creators

We believe the next great Sri Lankan service could come from anyone. Our job is to remove the barriers: no upfront cost, open APIs, clear documentation and a fair revenue share.

What we do

APIs, tools and AI on one platform

From raw telco APIs to no-code builders like AppMaker, AI services like Omni AI, and an MCP server for AI assistants, we give every kind of builder a way onto the network.
