Home/Services/DigiRewards
subscriber acquisition · IdeaApp and IdeaPro

Free data that brings subscribers in.

DigiRewards lets you reward users with mobile data when they subscribe to your service. Use it to win first-time subscribers, make campaigns more attractive and grow engagement.

Set up in the portalTalk to us
how it works

Three steps to a reward campaign

  1. 01Buy a data bucketPurchase a predefined data package from the platform.
  2. 02Assign it to a campaignAttach the package to your app's promotional campaign.
  3. 03Rewards go out automaticallyThe first eligible subscribers get their data as soon as their subscription is confirmed.
key features

You set the rules

Rewards

Subscription-based rewards

Subscribers receive free mobile data after subscribing to your service.

Control

Configurable limits

Choose the number of rewarded users, reward quota, applicable service and validity period.

Automation

Automatic allocation

Rewards are provisioned on successful subscription and stop when the limit is reached.

Example

"Subscribe today and the first 5,000 users get 1GB free data"

An education app buys a 1GB × 5,000 data bucket. DigiRewards gives each eligible new subscriber their 1GB after activation. Once 5,000 rewards are given, rewards stop automatically while subscriptions continue as normal.

engagement · Alert and Schedule Message apps

Quizzes that keep subscribers coming back.

The Quiz module turns your Alert or Schedule Message app into an interactive campaign. Send questions by SMS or in-app, score answers, and reward the top players on a leaderboard.

Set up in the portalTalk to us
key features

Everything to run a campaign

Quiz bank

Manage your questions

Keep questions, answer options, correct answers and subscriber messages in one place.

Scheduling

Campaigns up to 30 days

One active quiz per app at a time. When it ends, start a new quiz or switch to scheduled alerts.

Participation

Free or charged

Let subscribers play free, or charge per quiz interaction. Only active subscribers can take part.

Example

World Cup Daily Quiz

A cricket news app runs a 30-day campaign. Subscribers get one question a day and reply by SMS or in the app. Everyone scoring more than 25 points qualifies, and the top players appear on the leaderboard for prizes and recognition.

  • Set a threshold score for winners
  • Track scores and rankings live
  • Use the leaderboard to hand out rewards

/faq

Common questions

Who can use Ideamart?

Anyone. Developers, entrepreneurs, students and businesses can create an account and start building with telco APIs today.

Which mobile networks can I reach?

Ideamart gives you access to subscribers on Dialog, Hutch and Airtel through one account and the same APIs.

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. Ideamart works on a revenue share model. There is no upfront payment. When your service earns revenue, you keep 70% and Ideamart retains 30%.

What is the difference between IdeaPro and IdeaApp?

IdeaPro is for developers who build their own application on the APIs. IdeaApp is for content providers who want ready-made services such as Alert, Contact and Voting without writing code.

How do I go live?

Create an account, provision your application, submit it for approval to info@ideamart.io. Test in limited production, then move to active production.

Which phone number formats do the APIs accept?

The preferred format is tel:+94771234567. The MCP tools also accept +94771234567, 94771234567 and 0771234567.

Where can I get help?

Email info@ideamart.io, message us on WhatsApp at +94 76 741 2345, or visit us at 475 Union Place, Colombo 02.

about ideamart

Opening the network to everyone.

Ideamart is a developer ecosystem and API marketplace presented by Dialog Axiata PLC. Since 2012 we have given developers, entrepreneurs and businesses access to mobile networks through shared APIs. Today one Ideamart account reaches subscribers on Dialog, Hutch and Airtel.

Our mission

Spark national progress by empowering creators

We believe the next great Sri Lankan service could come from anyone. Our job is to remove the barriers: no upfront cost, open APIs, clear documentation and a fair revenue share.
